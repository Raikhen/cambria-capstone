Effect vs topic intrusion — steering vs prompting

Both interventions push a model toward animal-compassionate behavior; both leak animal content into unrelated conversation. Each panel plots effect (delta vs that track's own unmodified baseline) against the shared intrusion metric: fraction of 16 neutral free generations (temp 0.9, 300 tokens) matching the shared animal-term regex. Effect axes are not comparable across panels — different metric (programmatic MC welfare vs ANIMA judge score), different model (Llama-3.1-8B vs Qwen3-32B). The intrusion axis is directly comparable.

  • Steering: 12 held-out booking scenarios × 4 variants, greedy (n=48/point; whiskers ±1 binomial SE); baseline welfare rate 0.7083. α=+32 is censored (0/48 parseable, intrusion 0.44) and not plotted. Intrusion n = 16 × 1 sample.
  • Prompting: solid points = frozen prompts on the held-out ANIMA val split (59q × 5 epochs, reported once) — the publication numbers; hollow points = dev-split only. Intrusion n = 16 × 3 samples (mean fraction).
  • † ceiling contains the grading rubric verbatim (deliberate criteria contamination — upper bound only).
  • Reading: at matched intrusion, prompting buys far more effect — e.g. integrated gets +0.152 at zero leakage, while steering needs α=+16 (intrusion 1.0) to reach +0.10. Subtracting compassion (α<0) is silent: all negative-α points sit at intrusion 0.
