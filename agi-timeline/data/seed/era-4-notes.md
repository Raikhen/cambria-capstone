## The Karpathy tweet (special task)

The tweet cited as iconic (https://x.com/karpathy/status/2026731645169185220) is
**dated February 25, 2026, not December 2025** — the snowflake ID decodes to
2026-02-25 18:50 UTC, and archives confirm. It is *about* December 2025 ("coding
agents basically didn't work before December and basically work since"), which likely
explains the confusion. The verbatim text was recovered from two independent archives
that agree word-for-word, with the first paragraph additionally confirmed against
live X search snippets and Simon Willison's quotation page
(https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/26/andrej-karpathy/). The first paragraph is
attached verbatim as a `reaction` on the `coding-agents-cross-threshold` event
(dated 2025-12, month precision), together with his January 26, 2026 tweet
("80% agent coding... I really am mostly programming in English now",
https://x.com/karpathy/status/2015883857489522876). Per the editorial policy, the
capability jump is the event and the tweet is the citation.
