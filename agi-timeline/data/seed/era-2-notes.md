## Candidates considered and rejected

1. **FLI Puerto Rico conference / "Research Priorities" open letter (Jan 2015)** — cut
   under density guidance; the Asilomar principles (2017) are the more durable
   self-governance landmark from the same institution, and keeping both would be
   near-duplicate coverage of one development.
2. **Facebook founds FAIR (Dec 2013)** — ordinary business: a lab formation that did
   not shift who could build frontier AI the way the DeepMind acquisition or the
   OpenAI/Anthropic foundings did.
3. **DeepDream (2015)** — culture curio; fails durability ("only interesting this
   week") and counterfactual gap.
4. **Google Duplex demo (May 2018)** — explicit exclusion: demo without deployment.
5. **OpenAI Five (2018–19) and AlphaStar (2019)** — real capabilities milestones, but
   the self-play-games arc is already carried by DQN → AlphaGo → AlphaZero; low
   counterfactual gap, cut for selectivity.
6. **Megatron-Turing NLG 530B (Oct 2021)** — routine release: scale bump without a
   demonstrated frontier shift.
7. **DeepMind Gopher and Google LaMDA as model releases (2021)** — routine releases;
   LaMDA enters the timeline through the Lemoine sentience episode, Gopher through
   Chinchilla's comparison.
8. **Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights (Oct 2022)** — nonbinding white paper with
   little downstream consequence; fails durability and counterfactual gap.
9. **Yudkowsky's "AGI Ruin: A List of Lethalities" (June 2022)** — borderline; treated
   as a prediction/discourse piece per the exclusions. Yudkowsky's foundational
   writings belong to era 1's coverage.
10. **Ilya Sutskever's "slightly conscious" tweet (Feb 2022)** — a tweet is a citation,
    not an event; attached as a reaction to the LaMDA sentience event instead.
11. **GitHub Copilot general availability (June 2022)** — duplicate: merged into the
    June 2021 technical-preview event.
12. **WaveNet (2016) and Google Neural Machine Translation (2016)** — significant
    deployments, but superseded within a year by the Transformer; context, not
    landmarks, and the era is held to a selective bar.
13. **Partnership on AI founded (2016)** — institution with little consequence for the
    trajectory of the field; fails counterfactual gap.
14. **Meta OPT-175B and BLOOM open-weight LLMs (2022)** — the open-weights story of
    2022 is carried by Stable Diffusion, which had far larger consequences; these
    fail the counterfactual-gap test at era density.
15. **OpenAI Whisper (Sept 2022) and DeepMind Gato (May 2022)** — strong releases but
    no frontier shift (Whisper) / benchmark demo without adoption at the time (Gato).

    duplicate under "one development, one event."

## Low-confidence items and judgment calls

  paper; both dates circulate, this is the competition-result convention.
- **Google Brain cat dated 2012-06-26** (NYT coverage; the paper was presented at ICML
  that month and its arXiv preprint is from Dec 2011).
- **Asilomar dated 2017-01-08** — conference ran Jan 5–8, 2017; the 23 principles were
  finalized there and published by FLI shortly after.
- **China AI plan dated 2017-07-20** (public release); the State Council document
  itself is dated July 8, 2017.
- **Timnit Gebru event dated 2020-12-02** (her public announcement); most coverage ran
  Dec 3–4.
- **AI art prize given month precision (Aug 2022)** — the fair announced winners in
  late August (Aug 29 per several accounts) but coverage and virality came Sept 1–2;
  I could not pin one day confidently.
- **OpenAI source URLs** use the current `openai.com/index/<slug>/` form, verified for
  two posts via search and applied to the rest (original 2015–2022 `/blog/` URLs
  redirect there).

  March 2016), which blocks fetching; I verified and cited a fetchable page containing

  creative" (eliasberbari.substack.com). Both quotes are widely attested.
